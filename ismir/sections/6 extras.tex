\section{Ethics Statement}\label{sec:ethics-statement}

This work is severely limited by the dataset. Songs mostly conform to modern Western standards in pop music which is far from a holistic theory of music. Our work contributes to the ongoing globalisation of the dominant Western culture in music and beyond.

Machine learning is having an increasing impact on the environment through the data centers and energy required to train them, as well as all of the materials required to build advanced computers. We were limited by computational constraints in this work and so did not undertake any large scale experiments, nor massively increase demand for ever-expanding data centers.

Training on copyrighted audio is a fraught and difficult issue to contend with, especially for generative models. The audio used in this work is kept private for this reason. However, we did not train any generative models. We hope that ACR represents a field in music information retrieval that is free of many of the worries for artists surrounding generative models. We did use generative models in this work, but restricted ourselves to generative models trained on licensed music.
