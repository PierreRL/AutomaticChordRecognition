\begin{abstract}
Automatic Chord Recognition (ACR) forms an important part of music information retrieval because chords are a fundamental part of understanding many styles of music. Despite the significant progress of early models, the problem remains far from solved. In this work, we investigate why performance improvements have stagnated and revisit various methods of improvement. We also conduct novel research on using generative models as both feature extractors and a source of new data. Finally, we rethink how the model predicts chords in time by incorporating beat estimation.
